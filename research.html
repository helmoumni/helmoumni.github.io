Research directions

Black holes: from horizons to observations.

Four connected directions bring together thermodynamics, spacetime geometry, holography and dynamics. Analytical calculations, numerical modelling and statistical inference provide complementary ways to explore these questions.

Black-hole thermodynamics & generalized entropiesBlack-hole signatures & observational tests of gravityString theory, holography & black-hole geometryField and particle dynamics near black holes

01

Black-hole thermodynamics & generalized entropies

How do statistical assumptions and thermodynamic ensembles shape black-hole equilibrium, stability and phase transitions?

I study charged and asymptotically AdS black holes using generalized statistics, in particular Rényi entropy. This work examines phase diagrams, critical behaviour and thermodynamic geometry, together with topological descriptions of phase transitions and models of horizon microstructure.

Free-energy analysis · Thermodynamic ensembles · Rényi statistics · Topological methods

02

Black-hole signatures & observational tests of gravity

Which features of light propagation can distinguish spacetime models and be compared with observations of compact objects?

My research explores black-hole shadows, gravitational deflection and optical appearance in different geometries. Comparisons with Event Horizon Telescope measurements connect these models to observations, while accounting for the assumptions of the spacetime and emission models.

Null geodesics · Shadow observables · Gravitational lensing · Observational constraints

Representative publications

    Physics of the Dark Universe 47, 101731 (2025)

03

String theory, holography & black-hole geometry

How can black-hole solutions and holographic descriptions connect gravitational physics with quantum-field-theory observables?

This direction connects black-hole thermodynamics to the AdS/CFT correspondence and examines geometries motivated by string theory and M-theory. Topics include bulk and boundary descriptions of criticality, holographic heat engines, and the optical properties of higher-dimensional black holes.

AdS/CFT · Bulk/boundary thermodynamics · Higher dimensions · M-theory

Representative publications

    Annals of Physics 465, 169679 (2024)

    arXiv:2301.08321

04

Field and particle dynamics near black holes

How do perturbations and orbital motion encode the properties of a black hole and its environment?

I investigate charged-particle motion, magnetic-field effects and oscillation frequencies, including models of high-frequency quasi-periodic oscillations (HF-QPOs). Earlier work also examines quasinormal modes as dynamical probes of AdS black-hole phase transitions, and Hawking radiation. Bayesian inference provides a route from frequency models to observational constraints.

Orbital dynamics · HF-QPOs · Quasinormal modes · Bayesian inference
